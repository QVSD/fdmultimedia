Experiments

A controlled A/B assignment mechanism for one factor at a time. Assignment happens before any performance outcome exists, is frozen for the life of the Robot run, and is never influenced by analytics.

Experiment outcomes are descriptive in Phase 14A. No statistical winner or causal conclusion is calculated yet.
